Privacy policy

rclone-neuronas is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, to back up the owner's own files.

  • It uses the Google Drive scope drive.file, so it can access only files it created itself.
  • Files are encrypted on the owner's device before upload. No data is sent to the developer or any third party.
  • No personal data of other people is collected, stored, sold or shared.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
